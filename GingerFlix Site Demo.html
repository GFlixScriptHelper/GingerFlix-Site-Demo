The Studio

Small team, since 2021.

GingerFlix has existed since 2021 — it started as an Animation Studio and has since grown into a Game & Animation Studio, now operating as GingerFlix Studios (formerly GingerFlix!).

It's a genuinely small team — two people covering development, animation, and art — backed by a community that's been around since the start, active in some seasons and quieter in others.
